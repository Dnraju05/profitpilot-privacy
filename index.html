Privacy Policy — ProfitPilot Seller Calculator

Developer: DHARN Store  |  App Package: com.dharnstore.profitpilot  |  Effective Date: November 9, 2026

DHARN Store ("we", "our", or "us") built the ProfitPilot Seller Calculator application as a commercial/freemium utility for online e-commerce sellers. This page informs users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of information when using our application.

1. Local-First Data Storage (On-Device Product Calculations)

All product names, cost prices, selling prices, marketplace fees, GST calculations, and calculator drafts that you enter into ProfitPilot are stored locally on your device using Android Room SQLite and Jetpack DataStore. We do not upload, read, sell, or share your business product catalog or pricing data on external servers.

2. Information Collected for Entitlement & Quota Verification

  • Anonymous Device Identifier: To manage the 30-day free trial and enforce the daily save quota (5 new product saves per day on the Basic plan), the app computes a one-way SHA-256 cryptographic hash of your device's Android ID scoped to our package. No personal identity, phone number, email address, or hardware serial number is collected.
  • Google Play Billing & Purchase Verification: When you purchase the one-time ProfitPilot PRO Lifetime upgrade (₹199) or tap "Restore Purchase", the transaction is processed securely by Google Play Billing. We receive only the cryptographic purchase token and order ID from Google Play to verify your PRO entitlement with the Google Play Developer API. We never see or store your credit card, debit card, bank, or UPI credentials.

3. Device Permissions

ProfitPilot requests zero dangerous runtime permissions (no camera, location, contacts, microphone, or media access). It uses only standard network permissions (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) to verify Google Play purchases and synchronize daily save quota counters.

4. Data Retention, Backup & Deletion

  • User Control: You can edit or delete any saved product calculation at any time, or clear all saved products immediately via Setup → Clear All Saved Products.
  • CSV Export: You can export your saved product catalog to an Excel-compatible CSV file on your device at any time.
  • Basic Plan Retention Policy: For PRO users and active 30-day Trial / 24-hour Grace Period users, saved products are retained indefinitely on the device. For expired Basic-tier users, saved calculations strictly older than 30 days are cleaned up automatically after creating a verified local backup in internal app storage.

6. Children's Privacy

ProfitPilot is a business utility designed for e-commerce merchants and adult business users (18+). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
